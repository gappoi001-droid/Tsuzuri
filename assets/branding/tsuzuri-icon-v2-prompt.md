Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. PNG master preserved; ICO contains resized PNG frames at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixels.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one finished square desktop app icon for Tsuzuri.
Primary request: a restrained, exceptionally clean contemporary icon built around a single abstract binding stitch. The emblem is a continuous ivory monoline that bends into two interlocking rounded loops, arranged on a slight upward diagonal, suggesting a thread that joins two things. Give the thread one deliberate open end and one crisp interruption at the crossing. Design a compact, memorable, optically balanced glyph with broad negative space and beautiful curve transitions. The geometry should feel precise and calm, like an independent Japanese design studio's software identity.
Style: absolutely flat graphic design, two solid colors, sharp antialiased edges, no surface texture.
Composition: one centered ivory emblem on one deep muted violet rounded-square tile. The emblem occupies about 58 percent of the tile. Uniform substantial stroke, approximately 6 percent of the tile width; refined but easily legible at 32 pixels. Tile occupies 90 percent of the canvas. Rounded corners, front view.
Palette: tile #40375F, emblem warm ivory #F6F3EB.
Background: truly transparent alpha outside the tile, perfectly clean edge.
Constraints: a single icon asset, no text or letter T, no caption, no wordmark, no mockup, no gradient, no bevel, no 3D ribbon, no gloss, no shadow, no glow, no particles, no decorative details, no cloud or arrows or book illustrations. Do not draw a literal sewing needle. The loops and their whitespace must remain simple and readable.
